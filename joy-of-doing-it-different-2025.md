# The Joy of Doing It Different

As winter arrived in big steps, there was more time for the things that
matter in winter: light. Lighting is central for me, and I love playing
with it in the smart home, so I printed my own hallway lamp and built it
around an ESP32.

![3D-printed hallway lamp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LeonBurghardtDev/BlogArticles/main/images/diy_fun/floor.jpg)

An old 1.5L vodka bottle also turned into a perfect lamp once it was empty.
The pattern creates a real party atmosphere.

![Vodka bottle lamp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LeonBurghardtDev/BlogArticles/main/images/diy_fun/vodka_lamp.png)

And the highlight? Is it a PS3? Is it a computer? Is it a Steam Machine?
No, it is my self-built console. Before the Steam Machine was announced,
I grabbed an old PS3 case, older PC hardware, and installed SteamOS. With
emulators, games like Mario 64, FIFA 10, or GTA San Andreas play perfectly,
and of course it comes with the right lighting.

![Self-built console](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LeonBurghardtDev/BlogArticles/main/images/diy_fun/console.jpg)

Across all three projects I use ESP32 for control and seamless integration
into the existing smart home system. The RGB strips use WS2812 chips, which
let me control each LED individually, so cool effects are possible.
