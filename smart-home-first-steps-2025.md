# First Steps Toward a Smart Home

The plan for a self-built smart home was set, and we slowly got to work
building the foundations. We assembled a home server from old and new
hardware, built into two IKEA tables. In parallel, a new technical setup
emerged with a brand-new desk including a workbench.

![Homeserver und Aufbau](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LeonBurghardtDev/BlogArticles/main/images/smart_home/IMG_20250519_174847%281%29.jpg)

We put Shellys into the light switches to control the lights. The couch
now has built-in outlets (no worries about heat, we handled that). It was
every day at the hardware store and every day tinkering for our own home,
modular, not tied to the apartment, inexpensive and self-made.

![Werkbank und Infrastruktur](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LeonBurghardtDev/BlogArticles/main/images/smart_home/IMG_20250531_215512%281%29.jpg)

During this time I really discovered the joy of DIY projects and, above
all, learned how to turn ideas into reality: dive into new things, build,
test, and have fun.

![Schreibtisch und Technik](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/LeonBurghardtDev/BlogArticles/main/images/smart_home/IMG_20250616_191222.jpg)

By the way, I use Home Assistant to control our smart home. Everything
runs locally, and we now operate over 50 smart devices: from thermometers
to window sensors to self-built presence detectors. It makes our home feel
truly and sustainably smart.
