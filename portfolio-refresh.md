# Portfolio Refresh: From Good to Great

When the content is solid, the upgrade is mostly presentation:

1. Tighten the grid and lock a consistent card height
2. Add depth with layered shadows and soft gradients
3. Highlight the call to action with a strong accent color
4. Keep copy short and sharp

The goal is to make every section feel intentional and easy to scan.
